## Brand & Style

This design system expresses a high-density, mission-critical operational cockpit modeled after Apple's signature pro interfaces and optical glass surfaces. Built for retail operations leads, revenue analysts, and enterprise field commanders, it channels absolute precision, quiet confidence, and zero ambient noise.

The visual system supports dual operational environments:
- **Obsidian Glass (Dark Mode):** Optimized for low-glare operations centers, mission command dashboards, and nighttime analysis. Deep obsidian voids layered with low-opacity frosted acrylic sheets, razor-sharp 1px boundary rims, and ultra-deep ambient depth.
- **Ice Glass (Light Mode):** Optimized for mobile field reps and store associates operating in high-lumen supermarket aisle lighting. Pure white acrylic glass plates, ultra-soft diffused shadows, high-contrast typography, and crisp edge definitions.

Common foundations across both modes:
- **Signal-to-Noise Purity:** Structural chrome recedes completely into background surfaces, empowering high-chroma diagnostic beacons (system blues, compliance greens, telemetry ambers, and revenue-loss reds) to direct operational attention without cognitive fatigue.
- **Information Density:** Compact, data-forward layouts optimized for high-cadence triage, tabular data readability, and frictionless command dispatch.

## Colors

### Dark Mode: Obsidian Glass Palette
- **Canvas Base (`#0B0F17` / `#0F131C`):** Absolute dark foundation for all root viewport regions.
- **Glass Base Layer (`#0F172A` at 65% opacity):** Primary elevated glass panels, sidebars, and modular cards with `backdrop-filter: blur(24px)`.
- **Glass Elevated Layer (`#1E293B` at 45% opacity):** Interactive controls, child cards, nested table records, and hover states.
- **Precision Rims (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`):** 1px outer optical stroke for container edge separation against dark backgrounds.
- **Active Structural Rim (`rgba(10, 132, 255, 0.35)`):** Focused or hero-selected card perimeter stroke.

### Light Mode: Ice Glass Palette
- **Canvas Base (`#F5F6F9`):** Neutral cool light substrate providing optimal ambient contrast under fluorescent store lighting.
- **Glass Base Layer (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.82)`):** Translucent frosted cards and sidebars with `backdrop-filter: blur(20px) saturate(180%)`.
- **Glass Elevated Layer (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.95)`):** Interactive cards, hover states, and elevated inputs.
- **Precision Rims (`rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.07)`):** 1px outer boundary rim separating white glass cards from the light canvas.
- **Active Structural Rim (`rgba(0, 113, 227, 0.35)`):** Focused or hero-selected card perimeter stroke in Apple Blue.

### Shared Semantic Status & Diagnostic Palette
- **System Blue (`#0071E3` / Dark: `#0A84FF` / `#AAC7FF`):** Primary command actions, active routing indicators, selected navigational pills, and focus rings.
- **Emerald Green (`#008837` / Dark: `#30D158` / `#47E266`):** Target attainment, fully compliant inventory baselines, positive delta metrics, and connected real-time daemon statuses.
- **Amber Orange (`#B36B00` / Dark: `#FF9F0A` / `#FFB868`):** Telemetry lag, stale camera sync, missing audit payloads, and cautionary notifications.
- **Rose Red (`#BA1A1A` / Dark: `#FF453A` / `#FFB4AB`):** Negative sales deltas, urgent revenue leakage, critical stock-outs, and immediate dispatch triggers.

### Text & Iconography Hierarchy
- **Dark Mode:**
  - **Text Primary (`#F8FAFC` / `#DFE2EE`):** Store names, primary metrics, high-contrast modal headers.
  - **Text Secondary (`#94A3B8` / `#C0C6D6`):** Secondary metadata, store location breadcrumbs, sub-headers.
  - **Text Tertiary / Ghost (`#64748B`):** System timestamps, table column dividers, and inactive state indicators.
- **Light Mode:**
  - **Text Primary (`#0F172A` / `#181C22`):** Primary headings, store titles, high-emphasis metrics.
  - **Text Secondary (`#475569` / `#434751`):** Sub-headers, metadata, operational descriptions.
  - **Text Tertiary / Ghost (`#94A3B8` / `#737782`):** Timestamps, breadcrumb dividers, disabled elements.

## Typography

Inter serves as the foundational typeface, selected for its neutral precision, geometric rhythm, and exceptional readability at small sizes. All numeric displays, monetary values, and delta counters strictly enforce `font-feature-settings: "tnum" 1, "cv05" 1` (tabular numerals) to maintain vertical alignment across rapidly sorting rows.

- **Scale Rhythm:** Tuned for high-density enterprise displays, prioritizing sizes between 11px and 16px to maximize situational awareness on a single screen without scrolling.
- **Code & Logic Identifiers:** JetBrains Mono is utilized for algorithmic formulas, store IDs (`S001`), database sync timestamps, and raw JSON telemetry fragments.
- **Casing & Hierarchy:** System section labels and status indicators use uppercase or elevated tracking (`letter-spacing: 0.04em`) to act as visual anchors.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model enforces a balanced 12-column responsive fluid grid structured around modular operational pods:
- **Canvas Shell:** 24px outer margin (`margin`) with a top persistent utility status bar (44px height) providing cross-system telemetry and user context.
- **Data Pods:** Content modules conform to a 16px gutter (`gutter`), collapsing from a multi-column dashboard on ultra-wide screens to single-column card stacks below 1024px.
- **Internal Density:** Element-to-element gaps adhere strictly to compact 4px base increments:
  - `space-xs` (4px) for inline badge icons, status dots, and pill gaps.
  - `space-sm` (8px) for related metadata tags, chip clusters, and button-to-label bindings.
  - `space-md` (12px) for list row internal vertical padding.
  - `space-lg` (20px) for parent panel padding and card group boundaries.
  - `space-xl` (32px) for primary section demarcations.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is communicated via translucent optical materials rather than opaque directional drop shadows:

### Dark Mode Elevation
1. **Substrate (Level 0):** Unblurred raw background `#0B0F17`.
2. **Glass Plate (Level 1):** Main functional enclosures. Applied with `background: rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.65)`, `backdrop-filter: blur(24px) saturate(180%)`, and bordered by `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`.
3. **Glass Inset / Module (Level 2):** List items and interactive cells inside containers. Rendered with `background: rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.03)` on resting state, shifting to `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.06)` on pointer hover.
4. **Floating HUD / Flyout (Level 3):** Dropdown filters, modal dialogs, and floating command triggers. Backed by `rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.90)`, `backdrop-filter: blur(32px)`, bordered by `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.14)`, and cast with a diffused ambient shadow: `box-shadow: 0 16px 40px -8px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.60)`.
5. **Hero Highlight:** Active rows (e.g., top priority targets) utilize a dual-ring elevation: an outer `1px solid rgba(10, 132, 255, 0.40)` outline complemented by an ultra-faint interior ambient glow (`inset 0 0 16px rgba(10, 132, 255, 0.06)`).

### Light Mode Elevation
1. **Substrate (Level 0):** Clean neutral canvas `#F5F6F9`.
2. **Glass Plate (Level 1):** Main containers with `background: rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.82)`, `backdrop-filter: blur(20px) saturate(180%)`, bordered by `1px solid rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.07)`, and soft ambient shadow `box-shadow: 0 2px 12px -2px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.04)`.
3. **Glass Inset / Module (Level 2):** List items and interactive cells. Rendered with `background: rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.02)` on resting state, shifting to `rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.05)` on hover.
4. **Floating HUD / Flyout (Level 3):** Dropdown filters and modals backed by `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.96)`, bordered by `1px solid rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.10)`, with `box-shadow: 0 16px 40px -6px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.12)`.
5. **Hero Highlight:** Active rows feature an outer `1px solid rgba(0, 113, 227, 0.45)` border with a subtle inner blue tint (`inset 0 0 16px rgba(0, 113, 227, 0.04)`).

## Shapes

The design system incorporates Apple's classic smooth squircles and calculated radii:
- **Global Container Radii:** Standard cards, glass pods, and large content sections use `1rem` (16px) corners to frame high-density internal content.
- **Component Radii:** Individual list rows, input wrappers, badges, and contextual buttons use `0.5rem` (8px) corners.
- **Pills & Status Indicators:** System filter chips, connection beacons, and live status labels enforce a 9999px fully pill-shaped radius.
- **Optical Inset Rule:** Nested elements inside rounded containers have a radius equal to the parent radius minus the intervening padding, preventing awkward visual corner pinches.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Triggers
- **Primary Command:** Solid Apple Blue (`#0071E3`), white text, semi-bold font, `8px` corner radius, subtle hover brightening to `#0A84FF`, accompanied by an active chevron icon.
- **Secondary / Ghost:** Glass surface (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05)`), `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.10)`, with white text transitioning to pure translucent white on hover.
- **Filter Segment Tabs:** Pill-shaped glass container holding segmented toggle items. Selected state is filled with active blue (`#0071E3`) or elevated slate (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`).

### Metric & Status Chips
- **Telemetry Beacon:** Pill tag containing an active 6px animated breathing dot alongside micro-copy (e.g., `Firestore: Connected`, `Snapshot 36h Stale`).
- **Data Tag / Moniker:** Monospaced store identifier badges (`S001`) encased in an ultra-compact rounded rectangle (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.06)` background, `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)` border).
- **Delta Indicator:** Compact flex layout showing upward green or downward red trend arrow alongside tabular percentage figures.

### List & Radar Rows
- **Structure:** Horizontal layout comprising Rank Index (`#01`), Store Identification & Diagnostic Meta, Sales Trend Delta, Tabular Dollar Gap, and Action CTA.
- **Hierarchy:** Distinct visual differentiation between Hero/Priority items (illuminated border, higher opacity) and baseline compliant items (muted opacity, zero opportunity delta).
- **Separation:** Internal rows utilize 8px vertical gaps or border-less tonal grouping to maintain continuous scanning efficiency.

### Input & Search Fields
- **Search Command Bar:** Dark tinted glass pill or rounded rectangle with embedded search icon, placeholder text in `Text Tertiary` (`#64748B`), and active glow border on focus (`rgba(10, 132, 255, 0.50)`).
- **Selectors / Dropdowns:** Integrated caret, contextual count pills, and subtle border lines indicating clickable territory.

### Telemetry Alert Containers
- **Warning Alert Glass Pod:** Amber-tinted glass surface (`rgba(255, 159, 10, 0.05)`) with a low-opacity amber boundary (`rgba(255, 159, 10, 0.25)`), dedicated to ungrounded feeds, missing camera angles, or stale sync pipelines.
