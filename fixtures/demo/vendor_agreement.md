# Vendor Merchandising Agreement

**Agreement ID**: AGR-AQUAPURE-2026-09  
**Vendor**: AquaPure Beverages LLC  
**Retailer**: StoreOps Demo Operations  
**Effective Date**: 2026-09-01 through 2026-10-31  

---

### Section 1: Covered Products & SKUs
1.1 This agreement covers **AquaPure Sparkling Spring Water 500ml** (SKU: `BEV-WAT-001`).

### Section 2: Shelf Placement and Facings Mandate
2.1 In all participating stores, the retailer shall maintain a minimum of **2 primary facings** and a target of **4 facings** for SKU `BEV-WAT-001` on eye-level shelves (Shelf Level 3).
2.2 The covered SKU must be placed contiguous to related premium beverage lines and must not be blocked or substituted with competitor brands.

### Section 3: Pricing & Promotion Conditions
3.1 Base retail price is established at **$1.99 USD**.
3.2 Any promotional discounts exceeding 20% require written vendor authorization.

### Section 4: Audit & Verification
4.1 Visual compliance audits shall be verified by in-store field representatives using the StoreOps platform.
4.2 Non-compliant audits require remediation within 48 hours of notification.
