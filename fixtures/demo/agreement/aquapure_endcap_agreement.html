Shelf Placement and Promotion Agreement

Agreement No. AP-2026-FALL-014 · Fictional sample document for the StoreOps demo

This agreement is made between AquaPure Beverages Co. (the "Supplier") and Retail Demo Corp (the "Retailer") for the promotion of AquaPure Sparkling Spring Water in the participating store listed below.

1. Promotion period

The promotion runs from October 1, 2026 to November 30, 2026, inclusive.

2. Participating store and product

Store codeStore namePlacement zone
STORE-101Downtown Flagship #101ZONE-BEV-01 (beverage aisle, main shelf)
SKUProductPromotional price
BEV-WAT-001AquaPure Sparkling Spring Water 500ml2 for $3.00 (regular $1.99 each)

3. Merchandising requirements

3.1 The Retailer shall keep AquaPure Sparkling Spring Water 500ml (SKU BEV-WAT-001) on the main shelf in zone ZONE-BEV-01 for the full promotion period.

3.2 The Retailer shall maintain a minimum of six (6) facings of SKU BEV-WAT-001 on the main shelf in zone ZONE-BEV-01 at all times during store opening hours.

3.3 The Retailer shall display the Supplier's promotional price card ("2 for $3") on the shelf edge directly below the product.

3.4 Back-room stock of the product shall be moved to the shelf within one business day whenever the shelf falls below the facing minimum in clause 3.2.

4. Compliance checks

The Supplier's field representatives may visit the store and photograph zone ZONE-BEV-01 to confirm compliance with clause 3. Where a requirement is not met, the Retailer shall correct it and the representative shall confirm the correction with a new photograph.

5. Promotional funding

The Supplier shall fund the price reduction at $0.49 per unit sold during the promotion period, payable only for periods in which clause 3 was met.
